Indian Blood Donor Eligibility

Before donating blood at an accredited Indian blood centre, donors are screened for general health:

  • Age between 18 and 65 years.
  • Minimum body weight of 45 kg.
  • Haemoglobin level of at least 12.5 g/dL.
  • Minimum 3 months gap since last whole-blood donation.
