Request Workflow

Once submitted, every blood request is stored in the database with an initial status of Pending.

  • Blood bank administrators review urgency and required blood group.
  • Status updates to Approved, Rejected, or Fulfilled are recorded in the audit history.
  • For demonstration stock quantities, check the Blood Availability page.
