Request Blood

Submit a blood requirement for a patient admitted at an Indian hospital. Every new submission is saved with status Pending for administrator verification.

+91
10-digit Indian mobile starting with 6, 7, 8, or 9.

Request Workflow

Once submitted, every blood request is stored in the database with an initial status of Pending.

  • Blood bank administrators review urgency and required blood group.
  • Status updates to Approved, Rejected, or Fulfilled are recorded in the audit history.
  • For demonstration stock quantities, check the Blood Availability page.

Track Your Request Status

Register or sign in as a Patient / Blood Requester to submit requests from your account and track status updates in real time.
